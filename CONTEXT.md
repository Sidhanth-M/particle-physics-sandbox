# Particle Physics Sandbox

A falling-sand cellular-automaton toy where every material shares the same heat, charge, and density fields, so all behavior emerges from one coherent physics rather than per-material scripts.

## Language

### Vision

**Emergent spectacle**:
The moment the toy exists to deliver — a scene visibly answering a poke, or quietly running its own show, produced by shared physics rather than scripted outcomes.
_Avoid_: eye candy, chaos

**Physical plausibility**:
Behavior arises from the shared fields; nothing permanently injects a field quantity, except capped self-heating that its material can still lose — a store that fights back, never a source that ignores the world. Scripted pair reactions are reserved for identity-driven chemistry.
_Avoid_: realism, accuracy

**Discoverability**:
A player can predict what a material will do from tooltips alone and confirm it in one codex line.
_Avoid_: tutorialization

**Player instrument**:
An unlimited matter source treated as a tool aimed by the player, not as world matter — unlike field sources, it may be permanent, but only matter flows through it: a field quantity like flame or charge can never be poured forever.
_Avoid_: flamethrower loophole, infinite battery

### Redesign vocabulary

**Verdict**:
The keep/cut/change decision recorded for each material during a redesign effort.
_Avoid_: ruling, call

**One-job rule**:
Every kept material has exactly one distinct role; overlapping roles force cut, merge, or demonstrated re-differentiation.

**Consolidation**:
Merging all code, styles, and checks into one self-contained HTML file that is itself the source of truth — carried anywhere, edited directly, no generator.
_Avoid_: bundle, build

**Soil**:
Dirt's one job — the ground seeds sprout from; germination takes damp soil, not open water.
_Avoid_: filler, mud

**Quench**:
Violent cooling of molten silicate on water contact — how Obsidian is born, where slow cooling would have given Glass.
_Avoid_: freezing

**Snowfall**:
Steam condensing in freezing air falls as snow instead of water — how snow earns a production path.
_Avoid_: weather system
